Ardron Universe is a ... Fantasy/Science Fantasy setting spanning multiple games, stories, and timelines.

This is **not** a happy universe, and no, it's not an allegory for depression. It is simply the way the wheel turns.

Many of the stories explore themes such as death, grief, trauma, corruption, war, and morality. While there are moments of hope, this is ultimately a setting where terrible things happen to good people simply because this world isn't fair. It isn't just. '''It simply is.'''

The gods themselves are not perfect, omniscient, or all-powerful. They are simply doing their best, and their best is catastrophic.

If you gave 6 cosmic children absurd amounts of power...It would probably go the same.

Consider yourself warned.

Enjoy 😎
